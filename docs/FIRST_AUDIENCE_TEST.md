# RollinDD First Audience Test

## First audience

English-speaking small-business owners and creative founders who already use short solo walks or drives for personal-development audio.

This is a narrow, reachable starting audience. The current seven-part collection speaks directly to acting despite fear, creating, connecting, choosing wisely, collaborating, and balancing ambition with patience.

## Repeat-use goal

Invite five audience-fit listeners. The test succeeds when at least three of them return on a different day within seven days and listen to at least 60 seconds of a different production.

## Privacy-safe measurement

Assign each tester a random ID from `L01` through `L05`. Record only:

- first-listen date;
- whether the listener returned on a different day within seven days; and
- whether that return included at least 60 seconds of a different production.

Do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or browsing history. Until privacy-respecting in-app measurement is approved, use a no-name yes/no self-report tied only to the random tester ID.

## Current baseline

- Seven ordered productions are available.
- Playback, readable words, lyric search, and MP3 downloads are present.
- The v3 candidate addresses the reproduced first-tap playback reset caused by React reapplying the audio element's `src` after playback began.
- A real iPhone Safari pass is still required before calling the mobile listener test complete.
